Understanding Unofficial Results

* Results are unofficial until the Secretary of State certifies the results of the election. The official canvass is completed approximately three weeks after the conclusion of the election, and results will be released following the office’s review of the county boards of elections’ official canvass reports.

Outstanding Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act (UOCAVA) absentee ballots may be viewed by clicking the “Ballots Cast” tab. Outstanding UOCAVA absentee ballots include all absentee ballots requested by UOCAVA voters but not yet returned by the close of polls on Election Day. Outstanding UOCAVA absentee ballots, sent through the mail, have up to four days after Election Day to arrive at their respective county boards of elections to be counted.

Outstanding provisional ballots are ballots that the bipartisan county boards of elections will consider for counting during the official canvass process. Qualifying provisional ballots will be included in the final official results. The outstanding UOCAVA absentee and provisional ballot count will update as each county completes their Election Night reporting.
