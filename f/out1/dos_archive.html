Election Results Archive

Webpage last updated: September 15, 2023

The Division of Elections maintains an archive of current and past election results data. Data in the archive goes back to 1978 and it includes results from General, Primary, Presidential Preference Primary, and Special Elections. For information on local and municipal election results, please contact the respective County Supervisor of Elections Office. For information on election results prior to 1978, please contact the State Archives of Florida.
