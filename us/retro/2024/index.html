U.S. Senate

  
Democrats + independents 47  |  Republicans 53
100 seats; 51 for a majority. Includes seats not up this year.

Republicans won the Senate majority, 53-47. Of the 34 Senate races in 2024, Republicans won 15 and gained 4 seats (flipping Montana, Ohio, Pennsylvania and West Virginia); Democrats and independents who caucus with them won 19.

U.S. House

  
Democrats 215  |  Republicans 220
435 seats; 218 for a majority. Includes seats not up this year.

Republicans kept the House, 220-215. They won 220 of the 435 districts, 1 fewer than they held before the election.

Governors

  
Democrats 23  |  Republicans 27
50 governorships. Includes seats not up this year.

Republicans won 8 of the 11 governor races in 2024, with no seats changing parties; Democrats won 3. After the election Republicans held 27 governorships and Democrats 23.
